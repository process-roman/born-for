# Signs, Onboarding & Design Spec v0.3
**Companion to:** PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md v0.3 | **Date:** October 28, 2026

**What changed in v0.3:** "What do you enjoy?" became direction-first: pick worlds, then the jobs that catch your eye (§3).

**What changed in v0.2:**
- 8 attributes became **16 traits** in four families. Every sign has **8 signature traits** (predicted strongest, shown at the reveal and confirmed in the poll) and **8 background traits** (still valued by the sign, optional to confirm, trainable through quests). A Cancer who leads can now say so.
- Sign values come from one shared ladder, so every sign has the same 930-point total.
- "What do you enjoy?" grew from 8 to 12 options so every career world has a way in.
- A short optional "Anything else that's you?" step follows the poll.

## 2. Sign starting stats

### The ladder
Every sign ranks its 16 traits, and the rank sets the value. The same ladder is used for every sign, so every sign totals **930 points** and none is "better" than another. Minimum 35, maximum 85.

| Rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Signature** | 85 | 80 | 75 | 70 | 70 | 65 | 65 | 60 |
| **Background** | 55 | 50 | 50 | 45 | 45 | 40 | 40 | 35 |

- **Signature traits** (ranks 1–8 of the signature row) are the sign's strongest probable traits. The top 3 are starred at the reveal. All 8 are confirmed in the poll.
- **Background traits** (the background row) are traits the sign doesn't lead with, but they still have a value and count in career matching. They're never "zero": a Cancer starts with Leadership 45, Developing.

## 3. Onboarding questions
Three questions, all tap-only, no typing.

### Q1: Before the birthday — "What brings you here?" (single choice)
- 🔮 Discover who I am
- 🧭 Find a career direction
- 🔄 I'm thinking about changing careers
- ✨ Just curious about my sign

**Used for:** Tailoring copy and the home screen. "Discover who I am" leads with the character and attribute quests; career answers lead with paths. Also a useful analytics segment.

### Q2: After confirming stats — "Which worlds pull you?" (pick up to 3)
A grid of the 17 career worlds (PILOT_CONTENT.md §1), each with an emoji and a one-line blurb: Business & Entrepreneurship, Fashion & Beauty, Creators & Media, Gaming & Esports, Art & Design, Music & Performance, Writing & Journalism, Tech & AI, Sport & Fitness, Health & Mind, People & Education, Nature & Animals, Food & Hospitality, Build & Fix, Travel & Adventure, Science & Space, Law & Public Service.

### Q2b: "Any of these catch your eye?" (pick any, or none)
The jobs inside the chosen worlds, as chips grouped by world. Long shots are marked. Picking nothing is fine; the app still ranks every job in those worlds.

**Used for:** The interest part of career ranking (50% weight): a picked job scores highest, jobs in chosen worlds next, everything else only by similarity. The user's direction leads; the sign and traits decide order, difficulty and a couple of wildcards.

### Q3: "How do you like to work?" (single choice)
- 🧍 Mostly on my own
- 👥 In a small team
- 🌐 With lots of different people
- 🤷 Not sure yet

**Used for:** Work-style match in career ranking and as a reason shown on career cards.

## 4. Confirming traits: the poll
Instead of sliders, users answer one card per **signature trait** (8 cards, about 30 seconds). Each card shows what the sign predicts and a plain behavioral statement.

### Card layout
> **EMPATHY** · Your sign says: **Strong**
> *"You can tell how someone feels before they say it."*
>
> [ That's me ] [ Sometimes ] [ Not really ]

Swipe right = That's me, swipe left = Not really. "Sometimes" is a button. "Skip" keeps the sign's value.

**Order:** Top 3 signature traits first (most likely to get a "That's me", which feels good), then the other 5.

### Then: "Anything else that's you?" (optional)
One screen with the sign's 8 background traits as chips. Tapping a chip counts as "That's me" for that trait; untapped traits keep the sign's value. Continue works with zero taps.

### Statements
| Trait | Statement |
|---|---|
| Analytical thinking | You enjoy figuring out why something works, or why it doesn't. |
| Curiosity | You often go down rabbit holes learning about something new. |
| Attention to detail | You spot small mistakes or details other people don't notice. |
| Organization | You like having a plan and keeping things in order. |
| Creativity | You often come up with your own ideas instead of doing it the usual way. |
| Aesthetic eye | You can tell when colors, shapes or sounds go together, and it matters to you. |
| Courage | You'll try something new even when you might fail at it. |
| Independence | You'd rather figure things out yourself than wait for directions. |
| Empathy | You can tell how someone feels before they say it. |
| Communication | Explaining things to other people comes easily to you. |
| Teamwork | You do your best work when you're part of a team. |
| Leadership | In a group, people often end up looking to you to decide. |
| Hands-on skill | You'd rather build or fix something with your hands than read about it. |
| Physical energy | You feel best when you're moving: sport, training, being active. |
| Persistence | When something gets hard, you usually keep going. |
| Calm under pressure | When things go wrong, you stay calm and figure out what to do. |

### Scoring
Each answer has a target value:
- That's me = **85**
- Sometimes = **55**
- Not really = **25**
- Skip = sign value

**Confirmed value = 30% sign value + 70% answer target**, rounded (exact halves to even).

Examples for Cancer:
- Empathy 85 + "That's me" → **85** (Strong)
- Leadership 45 (background) + tapped on "Anything else" → **73** (Solid)
- Empathy 85 + "Not really" → **43** (Developing)

The user's answers outweigh the sign, but the sign still shapes the result. Confirmed profiles don't need to total 930.

### Result screen
"**Your sign called 6 of 8.**" Counted over the 8 signature traits. Show the sign's stats as a ghost outline behind the confirmed bars, so users see where they matched and where they're unique, plus any background traits they added.
- A match = the answer kept the trait in the same band.
- This is fun, shareable, and honest about where the user differs from their sign.

### Character sheet
After onboarding, the Character screen shows all 16 traits by family. Background traits stay dimmer until the user confirms them or earns XP in them.

## 5. Next steps after onboarding

**Full flow:**
Q1 intent → birthday → character reveal → confirm poll → "Anything else that's you?" → "Your sign called X of 8" → Q2 worlds → Q2b jobs → Q3 work style → **paywall** → career reveal → first quest

### After the paywall
1. **Career reveal.** Top 3 paths animate in one by one, each with difficulty (Natural fit / Some training / Challenging but achievable) and 2–3 reasons. "See all paths" below.
2. **Choose a path.** User picks one to start, and can save others. Each path card has Interested / Not for me / Unsure.
3. **First quest.** Three quests per path in tiers, so there's a clear progression:
   - **Taste** (about 20 min): e.g. sketch a better layout for one app screen.
   - **Try** (1–2 hours): e.g. redesign one full screen in a free design tool.
   - **Test** (real world): e.g. ask a designer one question, or show your redesign to someone and get feedback.
   The first quest offered is always a Taste quest.
4. **Reflect.** Three quick taps:
   - How much did you enjoy it? (5 emoji scale)
   - Would you do more of this? (Yes / Maybe / No)
   - What felt natural? (tap attribute chips, e.g. Creativity, Persistence)
5. **Rewards.** Quest XP, attribute XP to chosen attributes, badges, and an updated difficulty label if an attribute leveled up.
6. **Next recommendation.** Based on the reflection:
   - Enjoyed it → next tier on the same path.
   - Didn't enjoy it → offer a different path ("Want to try something different?").
   - Mixed → another Taste quest on a related path.
7. **Home screen.** Character with stats, active quest, path progress (Taste → Try → Test), saved paths, and recent discoveries.

**For "Discover who I am" users:** Same flow, but career paths are framed as "Paths that match your character", and home leads with the character. Later: attribute quests that train a single stat (e.g. "Persistence: finish one thing you've been putting off").

## 6. Design style: "Celestial RPG"
**Concept:** A night sky meets a fantasy game character sheet. Mystical enough to feel like real astrology, structured enough to feel like a game. Clean and readable, never cluttered.

All colors, fonts, spacing, radii and shadows live in one file, `src/ui/theme.ts`; `tests/theme.test.ts` checks contrast.

### Day and night
- **Day (everyday screens: home, careers, character, onboarding):** light cream background (`#F8F4EF`), white rounded cards with soft shadows, deep plum (`#3B2A4E`) for primary buttons and key text, lots of white space.
- **Night (magic moments: sign reveal, star lighting up, level up, path complete):** full-screen deep plum (`#2A1E3A`) with a starfield, gold and peach accents.
- **Accents:** peach / terracotta and thin gold lines. Peach is used only for fills and large text; terracotta only for large headings.
- **Accessibility:** all body text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) on its background.
- **Element colors** survive only as the character's element glow (fire `#F2784B`, earth `#7DB46C`, air `#7EC8E3`, water `#3FA7A0`).

### Typography
- **Serif (Cormorant Garamond):** headings, archetype names, quest names and titles.
- **Sans (DM Sans):** body text and career details.
- **Small tracked caps:** labels, tags and buttons.
- Rule: serif for the magic, sans for the facts.

### Recurring motif
- **Arch window:** a dusk-gradient arch with stars, a moon and a gold orbit (welcome, Home, character). Gradient placeholders until the illustrations exist.

### Key UI elements
- **Character card:** Trading-card / tarot-card shape with the archetype illustration, sign glyph, name and top 3 stats. Designed to be screenshot-friendly for sharing.
- **Stat bars:** Slim plum-tinted bars with a peach fill and a small-caps band label. A plum tick marks the sign's prediction (or day one).
- **Quest cards:** Serif quest name with a tag line like "TASTE · 20 MIN · +80 XP".
- **Constellations:** Gold line art on plum cards.
- **Difficulty labels:** Small soft-tinted pills, styled like game difficulty (one, two or three stars).

### Illustration
- One illustrated character per archetype: stylized, semi-realistic, tarot-meets-game-art. Not cartoony, not anime.
- Constellation line art as a recurring motif (backgrounds, loading states, dividers).
- 8 simple attribute glyph icons in a consistent line style.

### Motion and feel
- **Reveal:** Stars appear → connect into the sign's constellation → card flips to show the character. This is the "wow" moment; worth investing in.
- **Confirm poll:** Swipe cards with light haptics; bars fill as answers come in.
- **Level up:** Short gold burst on the stat bar, nothing that slows the user down.
- Optional subtle ambient sound, off by default.

### Tone of copy
- In-world and warm for the game layer: "Your quest awaits," "You leveled up Persistence."
- Plain and accurate for career facts: training needed, salary ranges, day-to-day reality.
- Never negative about low stats: "untrained," "room to grow."

### Voice and promise: borrowed confidence → earned confidence
The sign gives users a confident starting story; their own quests turn it into something they've proven. The app is bold about strengths and honest about outcomes.

**Say boldly:**
- "Your superpowers: Empathy, Persistence, Organization." (the sign's top 3 at the reveal)
- "Paths where your superpowers shine."
- "People with your strengths often thrive here."
- "Runs on your Empathy, one of your superpowers."

**Never promise:**
- That a sign guarantees happiness, success or a specific career ("you will be happy as a designer").
- That a low stat rules anything out ("Pisces can't do analytics").

**Confirmed by you:** when a reflection marks a trait as "felt natural" for the first time, the rewards screen says so:
- Superpower the sign predicted: "Your sign called Creativity. You just proved it."
- Trait the sign didn't lead with: "Your sign didn't predict Leadership. You found it yourself."

Confirmed traits get a ✓ on the character sheet.

**"That's you":** when a path reaches 3 lit stars and every reflection on it was enjoyed (4–5): "3 stars in UX/product design, and you loved every one. That's not your sign talking. That's you."

### References
- **Persona 5:** social stats (Knowledge, Guts, Kindness…) that grow through real activities. The closest game reference to this app's core mechanic.
- **Co-Star:** how modern astrology apps feel premium and minimal.
- **Duolingo:** clear progression and rewarding feedback.
- **Tarot cards:** card framing and illustration style for archetypes.

### Avoid
- Dense fantasy UI (ornate borders everywhere, parchment textures, hard-to-read fonts).
- Childish cartoon style, which would undercut the 18–25 audience.
- Too much text on game screens; save detail for career pages.
